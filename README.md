# Customer Engagement & Product Utilization Analytics for Retention Strategy
Customer Retention Analytics - simulated banking case study inspired by the [European banking](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/home/html/index.en.html) sector context, analyzing churn drivers using Python, EDA, and Streamlit dashboarding.

## Tech Stack

- **Python** – Used for data analysis and project development
- **Pandas** – Used for data manipulation, DataFrame operations, and CSV handling
- **NumPy** – Used for numerical operations and array-based data processing

## Dataset
The current dataset contains basic customer and banking information, including:
- **Customer ID** – Unique identification number of each customer
- **Surname** – Customer's surname
- **Account Balance** – Current balance maintained by the customer
- **Number of Products** – Number of banking products used by the customer
- **Credit Card Ownership** – Whether the customer has a credit card
- **Active Member Status** – Whether the customer is an active bank member
- **Credit Score** – Customer's credit score
- **Geography** – Customer's country
- **Gender** – Customer's gender
- **Age** – Customer's age in years
- **Tenure** – Duration of the customer's relationship with the bank
- **Estimated Salary** – Estimated salary/income of the customer
- **Exited Status** – Indicates whether the customer has left the bank
